How the colors work

Cards follow Magic: The Gathering's color rules. Each flavor and topping adds a mana symbol, and the symbols set the frame. Vanilla adds generic mana, shown as a number, and a double pump counts twice.

    One color gives that color's frame. Two or more colors give a gold frame. Only colorless flavors give a silver frame.

    Sodas: Diet Coke, regular Coke, and Sprite.

    Flavors: coconut, vanilla, blue raspberry, blackberry, cherry, lime.

    Toppings: whipped cream, blue Jolly Ranchers, boba pearls, cherries, peach rings, mint.

    Bring your own: anything you want to mix in is welcome.
